## AGPL-3.0

Baruch is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later
version** (SPDX: `AGPL-3.0-or-later`). In short:

- Everyone may use, study, share and change Baruch – for any purpose.
- Whoever passes Baruch on, changed or not, passes the licence and the source code along.
- Whoever offers a changed Baruch to others **over a network** also offers these users the source code of the
  changed version. That is the difference between the AGPL and the GPL.
- There is no warranty, to the extent permitted by law.

## Third-party components

Baruch uses free libraries by other authors. Besides `LICENSE`, every package contains the file
`THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt` with all included libraries, their version, licence, source and the licence texts.

## Content

Baruch ships no Bible texts, commentaries, lexicons or literature. It only reads what is on your own computer and
shows its licence note. What you write with Baruch belongs to you; the licence of Baruch does not cover your
texts.
