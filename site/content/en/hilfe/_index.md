This help explains Baruch in everyday language, from the first start to the finished book. Every page leads from
the simple to the special and shows step by step how it works. Almost everything can also be used without
JavaScript; where a way only works with JavaScript or only on your own computer, it says so.

**The quickest way to the first book:**

1. **Text in:** [start Baruch](/en/hilfe/erste-schritte/#starten) and
   [create a new work](/en/hilfe/erste-schritte/#neues-werk) – or [import](/en/hilfe/einlesen/) an existing
   text, also from [Word](/en/hilfe/einlesen/#word).
2. **Accept proposals:** [write in the typeset view](/en/hilfe/schreiben/#satzbild), let an assistant
   [review](/en/hilfe/assistenten/#pruefen) and accept or reject its
   [proposals](/en/hilfe/schreiben/#vorschlaege).
3. **PDF out:** look at the book in the [typeset preview](/en/hilfe/buchsatz/#satz) and output it as a
   [PDF](/en/hilfe/buchsatz/#pdf) or an [e-book](/en/hilfe/buchsatz/#epub).

The keyboard shortcuts for Windows, Linux and Mac are collected under [Keys and mouse](/en/hilfe/tasten/). How
Baruch is installed, set up and run permanently is described in [Installation](/en/installation/),
[MCP and assistants](/en/mcp/) and [Operation](/en/betrieb/).
